Sample Driving Recommendations

Median representative trip for each driving style, compared against the efficient profile.

Hard Acc Count
You recorded 69 hard acceleration events vs 19 in efficient trips. Rapid acceleration sharply increases fuel demand. Gradual, progressive acceleration reduces fuel consumption and mechanical wear.
Hard Brk Count
You recorded 74 hard braking events vs 23 in efficient trips. Hard braking dissipates kinetic energy that cost fuel to build. Anticipating traffic flow further ahead allows gentler deceleration.
Acc Max G
Peak acceleration (2.83g vs 1.00g in efficient trips) is high. Occasional aggressive inputs significantly raise instantaneous fuel demand. A lighter right foot during the first few seconds of acceleration makes the largest difference.
Idle Frac
Your engine idled for 29% of trip time vs 15% in efficient trips. Extended idling wastes fuel with zero distance gain. If stationary for more than ~60 seconds, switching off is worth considering.
Speed Std Kmh
Speed variability is higher than the efficient profile (29.6 vs 23.8 km/h std). Maintaining a more consistent speed reduces the repeated acceleration cost. On open roads, cruise control or conscious speed-steadying helps.
Rpm Mean
Mean RPM (1316) is lower than in efficient trips (1529). In combination with low speed, this may indicate short urban trips where the engine rarely reaches its efficient operating range. Consolidating short trips where possible reduces per-km fuel cost.
Stop Go Events
Your trip had 8 stop-and-go events vs 3 in efficient trips. Frequent stopping and restarting burns significantly more fuel. Where conditions allow, maintain a steady roll rather than coming to a complete stop.
Speed Mean Kmh
